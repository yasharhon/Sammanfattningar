\section{Fundamental Concepts}

\paragraph{Supervised Learning}
Supervised learning deals with sets of iid observations $(x_{i}, y_{i})$. The first element is called the data and the second is called the label, and these live in data space $\mathcal{X}$ and label space $\mathcal{Y}$ respectively. The data and labels are connected by a probability distribution $P$ defined on $\mathcal{X}\times\mathcal{Y}$.
